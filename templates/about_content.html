About Konundrum

Konundrum is the codename of our CryptoHacking team. Our mission: dismantle a worldwide criminal organisation known as 'Greeks', whose members all hide behind a Greek-letter alias.

The organisation is built as a pyramid. Every member knows only a few others, so the only way up the chain is to solve the crypto puzzles that reveal them. Combine the right two members and the one directly above them comes into view — one step closer to their leader, 'Omega'.

Work your way up from the bottom of the pyramid. Each stone holds several sub-puzzles; crack them all to unlock the stones above. Compare your progress with other agents on the Hall of Fame.
